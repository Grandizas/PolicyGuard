1. A panel appears on the page

On a terms or privacy page, and on any form asking you to agree to one, a small panel shows up in the corner. You do not have to do anything to get it.

Everything is in that panel. Use Show all to see every finding with the exact sentence it came from, and Show on page to jump to that sentence. Closing it with × keeps it away for that site.

2. The toolbar button has the same detail

Clicking the Policy Guard icon opens the same findings, plus the optional AI reading pass and your settings. The panel and the button show the same analysis — use whichever you prefer.

Cannot see the icon? Click the puzzle-piece 🧩 button in the toolbar, find Policy Guard, and choose Pin to Toolbar.

What it does without asking

  • Pattern matching runs on its own. It is instant, free, works offline, and never sends anything anywhere.
  • Nothing leaves your browser unless you turn on AI analysis, add your own API key, and press the button on a page.
  • Every finding quotes the document. If a claim has no matching sentence in the text, it is thrown away rather than shown to you.
Policy Guard is informational only and is not legal advice. It can miss things, and it is not a substitute for reading anything that matters to you.
