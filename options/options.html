Policy Guard

How it reads policies

Policy Guard always runs a pattern-matching pass. It is instant, costs nothing, and never sends anything anywhere. Deep analysis is optional and adds a language model on top.

Deep analysis

What this sends, and where

When you run a deep analysis, the text of the policy page you are on is sent to Anthropic's API and analysed there. Nothing is sent until you press the button on a page — there is no background uploading, and pattern matching keeps working without this.

Your API key is stored in this browser's extension storage. It is not encrypted. Anything that can read your Firefox profile — you, other software running as you, someone with your unlocked machine — can read it. Use a key you are willing to rotate, and set a spend limit on it in the Anthropic console.

Policy Guard needs permission to reach api.anthropic.com.

What you care about

Tick the things you want flagged. Leave everything unticked to see all of it. This filters what you are shown and pushes your choices to the top; it also tells the AI pass where to look hardest.
